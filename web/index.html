Dravidian NLP Low-Resource Principles
1. Subword Tokenization (BPE / SentencePiece)
Kannada combines root nouns with postpositions (e.g. ಶಾಲೆಯಲ್ಲಿ = ಶಾಲೆ + ಯಲ್ಲಿ, 'in school'). Word-level tokenizers encounter unseen OOV tokens for every grammatical case. BPE isolates the shared root from inflectional affixes.
2. Back-Translation (Synthetic Parallel Expansion)
Abundant monolingual Kannada news and literature is translated in reverse into English using an auxiliary model, expanding parallel data by +50% without expensive human labeling.
3. Indic Cross-Lingual Transfer & Regularization
Initializes shared embeddings from related Indic representations (IndicTrans/mBART). Incorporates label smoothing (0.1) and higher dropout (0.35) to prevent memorization and alignment drift on tiny corpora.
